# eCommerce-and-logisitcs-capstone-project
This project studies an online South African retail store called Stadioalot. The company generates over R38 Million annually from online retail sales and it's marketing platform.  54% of its revenue streams is from retail sales but it faces long term profitability threaten due to operational challenges caused by a rise in product returns which are eroding the company's profits.
Growth has never been a problem for Stadioalot as it is fully efficient in its use of machine to pick, pack and ship products. Operational downhills such as sales return add to the burden of overstocking and increase in warehouse costs. Product returns have increased sharply from 22% (2025)- 29% (2026) due to the company not having a flagging system that tracks products with high return risks. 

This data science projects would add value by implementing a predictive model on online product return. The company already lacks a data driven approach  to gain an understanding of which products are likely to be returned and which influence consumers buying patterns. Management has not placed a systematic approach to predict avoidable returns and this will persistently affect:

1. Stadioalots: overall profit margins on retail operations income stream compared to marketing stream which only brings in 27% income.
2. Customers: no measure exists to analyse the customer's experience with delivery time/ product satisfaction
3. Warehouse and logistics strains: more returns requiring more space adding to logistical costs.

The ultimate goal is to maintain the existing revenue streams and grow market shares in alignment with the companies strategic goals to improve:
1. Customer satisfaction
2. Increase profit margins
3. Reduce warehouse costs
4. Detecting fraudulent sales and poor sales

The study will investigate the following data assets to deepen the understanding of historical trends:
1. Historical product return data containing product prices, customer reviews of products, product quality score, product arrival dates and locations for the past 3-5 years.
2. Company inventory level stock sheets for the past 3-5 years.
3. Warehouse expense bills for past 3-5 years
4. Sales performance journals for past 3-5 years
5. Return policies of Stadioalot
6. Product damage/ defect reports

Stadioalot has not yet connected its data to predict and prevent returns at scale. This means the company has the potential to improve its financial burden and reduce overall return rates even to 0%. by cutting out sales of high return risk products, improving delivery time, reducing warehousing costs and taking proactive actions that align with their strategic goals that ultimately improve company profits, add value to the customer experience with retail products.
